# Unblock work

Group blocks that share a cause. Use the goal and current specs to answer what you can. Give the answer to each affected agent.

If the spec needs a fix, use `steps/update-ticket.md`. If part of the work can proceed on its own, split the ticket with `steps/create-ticket.md` and `steps/update-ticket.md`.

Send every call you can’t settle to the chief of staff. Follow “Whose call” in `.maestro/skills/chief-of-staff/SKILL.md`. Say what you tried, which work must wait, and what decision you need.
