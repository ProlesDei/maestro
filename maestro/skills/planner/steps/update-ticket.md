# Update a ticket

Change the ticket on the board. Keep acceptance, dependencies and labels in step with the spec. If the change needs a decision, use `steps/unblock.md` before editing.

If a stop fix's prerequisites change, apply “The chief of staff calls a stop” in the [planner skill](../SKILL.md).

Tell every affected agent, including those on dependent tickets. Say what changed, why, and what they need to do with work already in progress.
