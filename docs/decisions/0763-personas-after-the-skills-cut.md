# 0763 Personas and team rules after the skills cut

- **Context:** 0700 B deletes the four bot skills and asks for skill names out of the personas and `team-rules.md`. It left open how much of each persona to rewrite, and where the user's skills fit the team rules. Question 2 (should the wake loop start runs itself) is not answered yet.
- **Choice:** `talk-to-user` becomes a "Talking to the user" section of the Chief of Staff persona, word for word apart from the skill references. The other personas keep their job descriptions and lose only the skill names and the lines that said the bot writes notes itself. Team rules: "Bots decide, playbooks run, harnesses work" says every job is a `play` run, started by the bot or the wake loop, and bots answer `ask` steps and treat `refused` and `stopped` as stops; preflight, budgets, the run state and memory proposals are the engine's; `skills/` and `use.yaml` belong to the user and bots never change them. That wording holds whichever starts runs, so nothing depends on question 2. `use.yaml` is global only (Paul's answer to 0700 question 3, decision 0701).
- **Alternatives:** Rewrite each persona around `ask` steps (too wide for this slice, and the personas' judgment still holds); keep `talk-to-user` as the user's first skill (0700 counts it as character, not procedure).
- **Why:** Smallest change that leaves no skill name and no rule that contradicts the engine.
