# 0510 Improvement proposals are scout tickets the Chief of Staff owns

- **Context:** `improvement-loop` made `blocked` tickets that the Chief of Staff closed as Done the "no-code way", and had no evals, baseline or approval-by-risk step (G10.1, G5.2).
- **Choice:** The loop measures adopted changes first, finds one repeated pattern, classes it by risk, tests it on evals with and without the change, then adopts tuning directly with a baseline or files the rest as an investigation owned by `chief-of-staff` (`in_review`, then blocked with `Escalation (chief-of-staff):`). The Chief of Staff decides, then closes it as owner (0009). Changes to Maestro itself are proposed only.
- **Alternatives:** Owner = Operations Manager (then it closes its own proposal after someone else decides); no ticket, just a log line (the decision would have no record).
- **Why:** Whoever decides reads the report and closes it, and every rule on Done holds.
