# 0756 What the other slices must do before slice 5 lands

- **Context:** Slice 5 can't run alone: slice 1 runs it, slice 3 renders its prompts, slice 2 answers its runner requests, and slices 4 and 6 share its names. The review checked each one's branch, and the working trees of slices 1 and 3, against 0750, 0751 and 0755.
- **Choice:**
  - **Slice 1 (`maestro-play`):**
    - `run:` may be a list under `each:`. For each item the requests run in order, and the step stops at the first one that fails, leaving later items unrun. `.steps.<id>.results` holds one record per item that started, each the record of its last request. Release needs this for deploy, then smoke.
    - Keep these from its working tree, which slice 5 now relies on:
      - `hasPrefix` and `contains`;
      - `results` that fill in as items finish (plan-project's `after`);
      - an `each:` with no items, or all of them skipped, is `ok`;
      - `with:` on prompt steps;
      - every `ask` answer goes to `next` by default.
    - A `block` in a run without a ticket creates a `blocked` chore ticket for the playbook's role, with the escalation as an `Escalation (chief-of-staff):` note, the same as groom's `store_broken`.
    - The engine's release after `end` is a no-op when the last write already ran with `--release`.
    - `line` and `latestLine` take the last matching line (0746), and `.ticket.trailers` is `latestLine` for each key, so the two can never disagree.
  - **Slice 3 (templates):** `tickets.md` reads `with.answer`. `groom.md`'s fixes use 0755's named fields, not `args`. `tickets`, `plan` and `propose` take 0751's modes and reply fields, as the working tree already does for `investigate`, `feedback`, `curate`, `draft-setup`, `patterns` and `groom`.
  - **Slice 2 (runner):** the 0751 reply fields and `head <id>`. The `play --input` row is no longer needed for the Chief of Staff (0755).
  - **Slice 4:** 0740 says `end` finishes through `done:`, but 0750 and slice 1 make `end` stop without it. Slice 4's `on: … end` targets become `done`. Engineers' handoffs add the `author-role:` trailer (0751), last, per 0746.
  - **Slice 6 and ship/wave2:** ship/wave2 edited `groom-board.md`, `plan-project.md` and `housekeeping.md`, which slice 5 deletes, and its groom golden task. Take slice 5's side: 0755 carries the wave2 rules over. `evals/check.py` and the `playbooks/README.md` index are slice 6's.
- **Alternatives:** Rename slice 5's keys to slice 1's committed names (its working tree has already taken 0750's); have release deploy every environment, then smoke them all (production would ship before staging's smoke check).
- **Why:** With these, `maestro-play lint` passes on all 16 files. The review got 0 problems with the working trees of slices 1 and 3, except release's `run:` list, which slice 1 doesn't parse yet.
