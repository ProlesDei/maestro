# 0735 The prompt templates take the modes, inputs and reply fields the YAML playbooks use

- **Context:** Slices 3, 4 and 5 of 0700 were written at the same time. Slice 4's review moved its playbooks onto this slice's names (0745) and asked for a few more. Slice 5 kept the names it listed in 0751. Rendered with slice 1's lint against both, the 0730–0733 templates were missing 11 of slice 5's modes: `proposals` and `flagged` (curate), `setup-answers` and `dod-answers` (draft-setup), `measure` and `find` (patterns), `goal` and `escalation` (investigate), and `plan`, `report` and `escalation` (tickets). Most of their steps' `out:` fields weren't in the template's reply list, so each such run would retry and then block. `scan`, `logs` and `cursor` were required `with:` keys that no playbook passes.
- **Choice:** Slice 4's steps keep this slice's names (`sha`, `findings`, `baseline`, `decision`, `next_tickets`, the `guide` mode). Each template adds what 0745 asks for: `review` `secret` (a secret or a live hole is blocked), `plan` `example`, `baseline` `hotspots`, `gaps` and `broke`, `after` failing when the target isn't met, takeover restarts reverting on the same branch, and review reading the handoff's `branch:` and diffing `main...<sha>`. Slice 5's steps get 0751's modes and fields. `tickets` drafts `goal`, `criteria`, `pointers` and `after` (positions in the list) in place of `body` and `ref`, and drafts no `fixes`. `groom` gives `ticket`, `version`, `command` and that command's own fields, which slice 5 quotes one by one (0755). `propose` gives `proposals` and handles reverts. `scan`, `logs` and `cursor` become optional: a template falls back to a `scan` or `logs` step's `output_path`, or to `.cursor`. The `dod` draft is the whole `project-setup.md` with its "Definition of done" filled in, because both playbooks install it under that name. It starts from the installed file when there is one, never from an older draft in the bot's scratch folder. Scratch headings add `## Goal` and `## Measured`.
- **Alternatives:** Rename the fields in slice 5's playbooks instead (it already reads 0751's names in gates and notes); keep both names (two words for one thing).
- **Why:** Each template now replies with every field its playbooks gate on, so slices 4 and 5 merge on top of it without renames.
