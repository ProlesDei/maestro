# 0719 Slice 1 takes slice 5's asks from 0756

- **Context:** 0756 lists what slice 5 needs from the engine before it lands. The slice 1 review checked each against the engine and linted the 28 converted playbooks of slices 4 and 5 with it; `release.yaml` didn't parse.
- **Choice:** All of them. `run:` may be a list: its requests go in order and the first failure stops the step, the record is the last request's, and under `each:` it leaves later items unrun. A step that lets the run's own lease go (`--release`, or a move to `done` or `cancelled`) ends it, so `end` and `done` release nothing more; before, the re-read after the write reported the run `lost`. A run with no ticket that blocks creates a `blocked` ship ticket for its role (`Type: chore`) and posts the escalation there as `Escalation (chief-of-staff):`, the same as groom's `store_broken`, and names it in `reason`. `line` and `latestLine` take the last line (0718). The kept behaviours (`hasPrefix`, `contains`, `results` filling as items finish, an `each:` with no items or all skipped is `ok`, `with:` on prompt steps, `ask` answers go to `next`) have tests.
- **Alternatives:** Have slice 5 split release's deploy and smoke into two `each:` steps (production would deploy before staging's smoke check, which 0756 rejects); leave a ticketless block in the log (a failed housekeeping or improvement loop would reach nobody).
- **Why:** Slice 1 can land first and slice 5 then lands with no engine change.
