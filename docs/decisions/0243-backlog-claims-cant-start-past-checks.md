# 0243 A backlog claim can't skip the dependency and area checks

- **Context:** Track B lets a claim by id on a `backlog` ticket skip the dependency and area checks, because grooming does no work (0227, 0228). But the holder could then move the ticket `backlog → todo → in_progress` with its token, and nothing checked again. Reproduced with the CLI: two tickets with areas `src` and `src/x` both `in_progress` under live claims, and a ticket `in_progress` while its dependency was `todo`. Likewise `update --areas` let the holder of a working claim move its areas onto another live claim's.
- **Choice:** A transition out of `backlog` on a ticket with a live claim runs the claim-by-id checks first (dependencies `done`, no area overlapping another live claim's), and fails with exit `4` `waiting_on_deps` or `area_busy`. Moves to `blocked` or `cancelled` are exempt. `update --areas` on a ticket under a live claim, outside `backlog`, fails with `area_busy` when a new area overlaps another live claim's. Unclaimed moves and updates are unchanged: they hold nothing, and the next claim checks.
- **Alternatives:** End a backlog claim when the ticket leaves `backlog` (silently drops a lease the holder still holds); check only on `todo → in_progress` (the ticket already holds its areas in `todo`, so it would block others unchecked).
- **Why:** The checks guard the moment a ticket starts holding its areas. Leaving `backlog` under a claim is that moment, the same as a claim by id.
