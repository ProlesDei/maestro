# 0507 Triage hands all work to a Planner

- **Context:** G4.5: `triage-request` wrote engineer tickets, so the Chief of Staff worked at ticket level, which the brief rules out.
- **Choice:** Triage reads `summary`, then either answers or creates a `Plan:` ticket for the Planner, for a new or existing workstream, even for small asks. Requests outside the active bets get one question first.
- **Alternatives:** Let the Chief of Staff write small tickets directly (faster, but breaks the hierarchy and the owner rule in 0503).
- **Why:** One Planner session for a small ask costs little and keeps every ticket owned by its workstream.
