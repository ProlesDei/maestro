# Shape a goal

Name who the feature is for, what they need to do, and what gets in their way today. Say what the feature will cover and what it will leave out.

Describe what someone should do in the running app and what result they should see. Use `maestro-unblock` if you need a decision before you can finish the brief. Give the brief to `maestro-plan-goal`.
