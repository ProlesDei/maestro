# Close a goal

Run each check from the brief in the running app. Record what you did and what you saw. If a check fails, keep the goal open and give the result to the engineer who owns the work. Use `maestro-create-ticket` for work the plan missed.

When the checks pass, give the chief of staff the brief and the results for owner sign-off.
